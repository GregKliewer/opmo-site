How the engagement works

  • A flat monthly access fee for a capped seat, not a day rate. No timesheets: you're buying a standing seat and availability, not metered hours.
  • Advisory Seat — one day a week. A seat at the governance table and judgment on call, without workstream ownership. For a board or exec team that needs counsel, not delivery.
  • Embedded Lead — two days a week, most common. A standing seat in your governance forum, embedded with the leadership team, owning a named workstream.
  • Transformation Lead — two and a half days a week. The embedded seat plus accountability for the operating-model outcome, and for training the person who inherits it.
  • Three-month initial term, then month to month on thirty days' notice, with a clear mandate and named outcomes agreed up front.
  • Remote-first with a regular on-site rhythm. Engaged as an incorporated independent contractor.
  • Available to start within two weeks. Terms finalized on a short scoping call.
