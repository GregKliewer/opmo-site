Rate by engagement shape

What each seat costs.

A day is 7.5 hours. These are starting rates for engagements up to six months; the term adjustments below apply on top.

Embedded Lead $2,400 CAD/day

A named workstream owned end to end, embedded with the leadership team, with a standing seat in the governance forum. Accountable for the workstream, not for the operating model around it.

Transformation Lead $2,800 CAD/day

The embedded seat plus accountability for the operating-model outcome, and for training the person who inherits it. This is the seat where the uncomfortable calls are mine to make and mine to carry out of the building.

AI-native operating model work $3,200 CAD/day and up

Engagements where the mandate is specifically redesigning an organization to absorb and harness AI tooling. This is where my current R&D time goes, and it prices at the top of the market because that market is young and the people who have actually run it are few. That’s a claim about scarcity, not about me being special; the two just happen to line up right now.

The rest of the terms

Adjustments, minimums, and what a day means.

Term adjustmentsSix months or more: 5 to 12 percent off the day rate, settled at contracting. Twelve months or more: 12 to 18 percent off, settled at contracting. The band inside each adjustment depends on commitment shape: a guaranteed 4-day week at twelve months earns the deep end, a loose 2-day retainer doesn’t.
Minimum engagementThree months. Shorter diagnostics are a different offering with a flat fee, and they run through the client-facing side of the practice rather than through this page.
ExpensesBilled at cost, no markup. Travel within the GTA is on me. Travel beyond it, and any client-requested travel, bills at cost with prior approval. No per diems, no grossing up.

The definition of a day, and why I state it

A day is 7.5 hours. Some firms post day rates against 8. On a $2,400 CAD day the difference is 6.7 percent of the fee, which is real money that both sides should be looking at rather than discovering.
